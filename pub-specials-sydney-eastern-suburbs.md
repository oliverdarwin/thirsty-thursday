## Data Quality Notes

- **Data freshness:** All specials sourced from EatDrinkCheap (updated within the last 1–3 months as of March 2026), official pub websites, and Merivale events pages.
- **IMAGE_ONLY specials:** SJ's Hotel Darlinghurst specials are primarily posted on Facebook/Instagram as images. East Village Hotel some day-specific specials (Thursday $1 wings, Friday $15 Friday) are image tiles on their website.
- **Dove and Olive (Surry Hills):** Website is image-heavy; no text-based specials found — visit doveandolive.com.au/whats-on/ directly.
- **Bayside Bistro & Bar (Double Bay):** IHG hotel restaurant; no traditional pub specials format. Closed Mon–Tue. Check website for seasonal offers.
- **Paddo Inn (Paddington):** Happy Hour not currently running. Focus is entertainment (live music, DJs). Food specials not prominently listed.
- **Royal Oak Double Bay:** Rotating weekly chef specials rather than fixed pub-night deals (no "schnitzel Monday" structure).
- **Always verify with venue:** Prices and schedules change. Always confirm before visiting.
